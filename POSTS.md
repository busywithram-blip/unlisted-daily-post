# Example WhatsApp Group Posts & Guidelines

## Posting Rules
1. Post at most one post per day.
2. Tailor example cities/niche details if needed when posting to local groups.
3. Never paste URLs or links in public WhatsApp group messages.
4. Post 1-2 times per group per week.
5. When someone replies with their niche and city, DM them directly.
